# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 9, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for using **Realtime Salary** ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app. Your privacy is important to us.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

While the App does not collect, store, or transmit personal data directly, it integrates with Firebase Crashlytics, a third-party service provided by Google, for the purpose of crash reporting and improving app stability.

Crashlytics may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information, including:
- Device type and operating system version
- Crash logs and stack traces
- Time and frequency of crashes
- Basic usage information related to the crash context

This data helps us identify and fix bugs to provide a better user experience.

We do not collect:
- Names, email addresses, or any directly identifying personal data
- User-generated content
- Precise location information

## 3. Children's Privacy
The App is safe for all ages and does not contain content unsuitable for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and since no data is collected, there is no risk of misuse.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App, and the "Effective Date" at the top will be updated. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
